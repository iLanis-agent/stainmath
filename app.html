The wood

Railing counts at 2.5 sq ft per linear foot (rails and balusters, both faces), stairs at 4 sq ft a step, posts at 2 sq ft each. Zero out what you do not have.

The stain

Coverage on the can assumes new smooth wood - weathered wood drinks nearly double. The second coat goes on while the first is still wet and covers about 50% more ground, because the wood is no longer thirsty.

Your order

Material only. Cleaner/brightener, a sprayer or pads, tape, and the plastic for the plants are separate - and none of this works over a dirty deck. The prep day is not optional.

What the can will not tell you

"Up to 400 sq ft per gallon" is new-wood marketinggray weathered boards drink like a sponge; plan on half the label number or you will run dry on Sunday afternoon
Two thin coats beat one thick onea flooded first coat peels by spring; penetrating stain wants to soak in, not sit on top
Wet-on-wet is the pro second coatthe second coat goes on before the first dries - wait a day and the first coat seals the wood against the second
More stain is not more protectionwhatever has not soaked in after 30 minutes gets wiped off, or it cures into shiny sticky patches
Solid stain is paint in disguiseit films, it peels, and it means scraping next time; semi-transparent lets the wood be wood
Rain winsyou need 24-48 dry hours after application and a dry deck before it - staining into a forecast is donating the job to the weather
